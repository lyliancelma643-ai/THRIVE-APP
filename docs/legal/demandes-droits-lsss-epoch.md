# Demandes de droits — LSSS et EPOCH

Deux courriels prêts à envoyer depuis `confidentialite@thrivesportpositive.com` (ou l'adresse du dirigeant). Ils sont rédigés en anglais, langue des auteurs. Joindre, si possible, une capture de l'écran du questionnaire dans THRIVE.

Avant l'envoi :
- **LSSS** : l'adresse indiquée est celle du profil de Lorcan Cronin chez Mary Immaculate College (Limerick). L'article de 2017 donnait une ancienne adresse à Edge Hill University. Vérifiez sur sa page actuelle.
- **EPOCH** : la page « Questionnaires » de peggykern.org demande d'abord de **s'inscrire** (formulaire en ligne), puis de contacter le Wellbeing Lab pour un usage commercial ; l'Université de Pennsylvanie (transfert de technologie) peut aussi être l'interlocuteur. Remplissez le formulaire, puis envoyez le courriel 2 à l'adresse qu'il indique.

Suivi : cocher le résultat dans `docs/legal/ip-registry.md` §4 et archiver les réponses.

## Courriel 1 — Life Skills Scale for Sport (LSSS)

**À :** Dr Lorcan Cronin — Lorcan.Cronin@mic.ul.ie
**Cc :** Dr Justine Allen (co-autrice ; adresse à confirmer)
**Objet :** Permission request — commercial use and French translation of the Life Skills Scale for Sport (LSSS)

> Dear Dr Cronin,
>
> I am writing on behalf of Thrive Sport Positive, a Québec-based company that runs THRIVE, a 13-session psychoeducational program that uses sport to support young people aged 8 to 17. Parents follow the program through a web application and mobile apps (App Store and Google Play), and coaches supervised by our team deliver the sessions.
>
> We would like to use the Life Skills Scale for Sport (Cronin & Allen, 2017, *Psychology of Sport and Exercise*) to measure the life skills of the young people we support. Our intended use:
>
> - **Instrument:** the full 43-item scale, eight subscales, with the original response format and scoring.
> - **Language:** a French translation we produced for Québec families, which we would be glad to send you for review. An English version would be used as published.
> - **Administration:** completed by the young person on their parent's device at three points of the program (start, middle, end). Results are shared with the parent and the assigned coach only, as a guidance tool and never as a clinical assessment.
> - **Context:** a paid service (family subscription). The scale itself is not sold separately.
> - **Attribution:** full citation in the app and in our documentation, plus any wording you request.
> - **Data:** stored in Canada; no individual data is shared outside the family, the coach and our team. We would be happy to discuss anonymised aggregate results if they are of interest to your research.
>
> Could you let us know whether you grant permission for this commercial use and for our French translation, and under which conditions (licence, fee, attribution, review of the translation)?
>
> Thank you very much for your work on this instrument.
>
> Kind regards,
>
> [Prénom Nom], [Fonction]
> Thrive Sport Positive — Québec, Canada
> confidentialite@thrivesportpositive.com · https://thrivesportpositive.com

## Courriel 2 — EPOCH Measure of Adolescent Well-Being

**À :** Prof. Margaret L. Kern — via l'adresse du Wellbeing Lab indiquée après l'inscription sur peggykern.org/questionnaires.html
**Objet :** Commercial licence request — EPOCH Measure of Adolescent Well-Being (French translation, youth sport program)

> Dear Professor Kern,
>
> I am writing on behalf of Thrive Sport Positive, a Québec-based company that runs THRIVE, a 13-session psychoeducational program that uses sport to support young people aged 8 to 17, through a web application and mobile apps (App Store and Google Play). We have registered on your website as requested.
>
> We would like to use the EPOCH Measure of Adolescent Well-Being (Kern, Benson, Steinberg & Steinberg, 2016, *Psychological Assessment*) in a **commercial** setting, and we understand this requires your permission or a licence. Our intended use:
>
> - **Instrument:** the 20-item final version, five subscales, original 1-to-5 response scale, subscale means.
> - **Language:** English as published, and a French translation we produced for Québec families, which we would be glad to submit for your review.
> - **Administration:** completed by the young person on their parent's device after program sessions. Results are shown to the parent and the assigned coach only, as a well-being guidance tool and never as a clinical assessment or diagnosis.
> - **Context:** included in a paid family subscription; the measure is not sold separately.
> - **Attribution:** full citation in the app and our documentation, plus any wording you require.
> - **Data:** stored in Canada; no individual data is shared outside the family, the coach and our team.
>
> Could you tell us the terms of a commercial licence (fee, duration, territory, conditions on translation and display), or redirect us to the right office (Wellbeing Lab or the University of Pennsylvania's technology transfer office)?
>
> Thank you for making this measure available.
>
> Kind regards,
>
> [Prénom Nom], [Fonction]
> Thrive Sport Positive — Québec, Canada
